
# 01. Industry Analysis

## Overview

This section focuses on analyzing the global automotive
industry and the strategic decisions of major manufacturers.

The objective is to understand how market conditions,
government policies, trade regulations, and regional
demand influence corporate supply chain strategies.

## Research Topics

- Global Automotive Manufacturing Strategy
- Regional Production and Localization
- Trade Policies and Tariff Impacts
- Automotive Industry Trends
- Manufacturing Location Decisions
